Free migration

From Bins-USA Lite to WMS Cloud in one click

When your warehouse outgrows Lite, WMS Cloud brings all your work with you. The migration costs nothing.

  • Your bins, the products in each bin and their quantities move in one click.
  • Your Lite labels keep scanning: no reprinting.
  • A backup is made before anything changes, and you can undo it.
  • Lite is not touched, and your Shopify quantities do not change.
  • WMS Cloud Pro free for 14 days.
  1. Install WMS Cloud first, on the same Shopify store that has Lite.
  2. Open WMS Cloud and click “Bring your bins from Bins-USA Lite”. Look at the preview, then import.
  3. Keep Lite for about a week while you try it. Then uninstall Lite so Shopify stops billing you for it (its data is deleted 48 hours later).
