Features

Everything your warehouse needs

From scanning barcodes to generating purchase orders — Bins-USA has you covered.

📦

Bin Management

Give every product a physical home — a shelf, a box, a rack. Create hundreds of bins from a pattern instead of typing them, and print the whole rack of QR labels in one go.

Bulk tools: Basic
🔍

Barcode Scanning

Scan a code — or type part of a product name — and see which bin it is in. Works with any barcode scanner or your phone camera. If it is stored at another location, it tells you which.

📋

Pick Lists

Every unfulfilled Shopify order becomes a pick list showing which bin to take each item from. Scan to check items off, print it for your team, then deduct from the bins in one press.

📥

Receive & Put Away

Pick a bin once, then scan item after item straight into it. It is how bins get loaded — at setup and when a delivery arrives — and it is many times faster than adding products one at a time.

🔄

Cycle Count

Count a bin and send the differences to Shopify. The app names the bins nobody has checked in a while, so counting stops being a chore with no starting point.

🧩

Bin Numbers Inside Shopify

Add the Bins-USA block to your Shopify order page and every order shows which bin each item is in — without leaving Shopify or opening the app.

📊

Reports

Inventory value by bin, with charts. Print it or export any report to Excel and know exactly what your warehouse is worth.

📉

Advanced Reports

Inventory rotation, sales by product with margins, active bins, dead stock and a full transfer history. Spot what is not moving before it sits.

⭐ Plus
👥

Users & Roles

Your warehouse staff scan, pick, count and receive — without seeing costs, margins or billing. Shop-floor mode keeps a shared tablet on their screen until a manager unlocks it.

⭐ Pro
📈

Sales Forecast

Analyze sales velocity and get suggested order quantities from your own history. See what is going to run out before it does.

⭐ Pro
📝

Purchase Orders

Raise POs straight from your forecast and track them from draft to received. Print each one in English or Spanish — work in your language, send theirs.

⭐ Pro
🏭

Vendor Management

Keep supplier contacts, costs, payment terms and lead times with the products they supply. Import a vendor's whole catalogue from Shopify in one click.

⭐ Pro
🌎

English and Spanish

One button switches the whole app — menus, screens, reports, the user guide and the help assistant. Included on every plan, free ones too.
